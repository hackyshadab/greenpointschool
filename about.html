Our story

Education should open doors.

Established in 2016, Green Point School is a private English-medium school committed to making quality education accessible to every child.

We believe that a child's educational opportunities should not be limited by financial circumstances. Our aim is to create an inclusive learning environment where children from different backgrounds learn together, grow together and feel equally valued.

With small class sizes, experienced teachers and activity-based learning, we focus on giving every child the attention and encouragement they need to develop academically, socially and personally.

Our goal is not only to help children perform well in academics, but also to develop confidence, practical skills, discipline, creativity and good values.

“

Our Vision

To create a learning environment where financial background does not become a barrier to a child's education and every child gets an equal opportunity to learn and grow.
